Powering Performance.
Delivering Trust.

With over 35 years of proven excellence, Atlasco Egypt has been a trusted partner in serving the industrial sector, consistently delivering reliable, high-performance solutions across diverse industries.

At the core of our strength lies one of the most effective and dependable maintenance systems in the country — built on advanced technologies and certified expertise in specialized tools from the Netherlands and Sweden.

Driven by innovation, Atlasco has expanded into the medical sector, proudly supporting governmental and private hospitals with advanced Medical Gas Systems that protect and save lives.
