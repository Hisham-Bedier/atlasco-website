What Is A.R.M.S.?

Advanced Reliability Monitoring System

A.R.M.S. is Atlasco Egypt's flagship managed reliability service — a structured, data-driven program that continuously monitors your rotating and stationary assets using multiple complementary predictive technologies. Unlike one-off surveys, A.R.M.S. is a sustained partnership that turns raw data into actionable maintenance intelligence, quarter after quarter.

Our dedicated reliability engineers visit your facility on agreed schedules, collect multi-technology data, analyse trends, and issue clear work-order recommendations — all measured against agreed KPIs so you always know the ROI.

The Technologies

OUR SPECIALIZED RELIABILITY SERVICES

Vibration Analysis

High-frequency spectrum and waveform analysis using Emerson & SKF class instruments. Detects bearing defects, imbalance, misalignment, looseness, and resonance — often weeks before failure.

  • ISO 10816 / 20816 compliance
  • Route-based & online monitoring
  • ODS & modal analysis

Thermography

FLIR thermal cameras reveal overheating bearings, electrical hot spots, refractory failures, and insulation voids. All surveys delivered as ISO-compliant thermographic reports.

  • Level II certified thermographers
  • Electrical & mechanical surveys
  • ASHRAE reporting format

Laser Alignment

Precision shaft and belt alignment using Fixturlaser and Pruftechnik laser systems. Poor alignment is responsible for up to 50% of bearing failures — we eliminate it.

  • Soft foot correction
  • Thermal growth compensation
  • As-found / as-left reporting

Oil Analysis

Laboratory-grade oil sampling identifies wear metals, contamination, and viscosity breakdown. Combined with our filtration products, we optimize lubricant life and gearbox health.

  • ICP spectrometry for wear metals
  • ISO 4406 particle counting
  • Lubrication optimization reports

Ultrasound Inspection

Airborne and structure-borne ultrasound for bearing lubrication verification, steam trap condition, compressed air leak detection, and electrical discharge identification.

  • Bearing lubrication trending
  • Compressed air & steam leak surveys
  • Electrical corona detection

Structural Integrity

Non-destructive testing including UT thickness measurement, MPI crack detection, and visual inspection — providing a complete picture of vessel, pipe, and structural asset health.

  • UT wall-thickness mapping
  • Corrosion trending
  • API 510/570/653 compatible reports
The A.R.M.S. Journey

The A.R.M.S. Implementation Lifecycle

01

Technical Audit

A lead reliability engineer conducts a comprehensive facility audit — cataloguing all critical assets, current failure history, and maintenance data — to build your custom A.R.M.S. scope.

02

Deep Analysis

Baseline data collection across all six technologies establishes the health fingerprint for every asset in scope, with initial fault findings and prioritised corrective actions.

03

Implementation

Corrective actions are executed, monitoring routes are established, and — where applicable — online sensor networks are installed. Your team is trained on the reporting portal.

04

24/7 Monitoring

Ongoing scheduled surveys, continuous online monitoring, monthly KPI meetings, and an annual programme review keep your reliability programme evolving — and your assets running.
