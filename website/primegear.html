ABOUT PRIMEGEAR ACADEMY

Where Industrial Knowledge Becomes Certified Skill.

Founded in 2012 by Atlasco Egypt, Primegear Academy was established to address a critical skills gap in Egypt's industrial sector — the absence of formally certified, practically trained reliability engineers. Over the past twelve years, the Academy has grown into Egypt's most respected reliability training institution, certifying more than 1,200 engineers from over 200 companies.

All Primegear Academy courses are aligned with ISO 18436, the international standard for condition monitoring and diagnostics of machines. Our instructors are certified at Category III or higher and bring real-world field experience to every classroom session — making our training exceptionally practical and immediately applicable.

Training is available as open public courses at our El-Obour facility, or as dedicated in-company programs delivered at your site — customised to your equipment and operating environment.
